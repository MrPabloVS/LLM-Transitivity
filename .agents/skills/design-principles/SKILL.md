# Design principles

- **Modularity:** components with a clear responsibility and interface.
- **High cohesion:** keep rules that change for the same reason together.
- **Separation of responsibilities:** distinguish domain, persistence, execution, integrations, and interface.
- **Abstraction:** expose useful operations and hide internal details.
- **Low coupling:** explicit dependencies and limited access to the necessary state.
